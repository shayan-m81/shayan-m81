## Work

At [Digikala](https://www.digikala.com) (Iran's largest e-commerce company) since 2024, I work full-stack on the internal Talent and Performance Management products used by 2,000+ employees. I was recognised as a High Performer (top 10% of the department) in the H1 1405 (spring–summer 2026) performance cycle.

- **AI features:** designed and built a shared AI gateway that both AI features use (structured-output validation, logging of prompt version, model, tokens and latency, and a kill switch); built, frontend and backend, an AI feedback-writing assistant that was used in about 500 questionnaires in its first week; sole backend author of an AI evaluation-summary feature (asynchronous generation through a job queue, versioned history, 383 tests) and its admin frontend; evaluating LLM providers on output quality, cost, availability and reliability.
- **Workflows end to end:** designed and built a promotion workflow with a stage engine, an append-only case history, review flows for employees, managers and committees, and an HR/ERP integration; delivered a performance check-ins module in about two days, splitting the work with a newly joined teammate.
- **Product features:** the 360-degree feedback report, the OKR experience in the user and admin panels, evaluation flows, Persian/English dictation inside the editor, and, built on my own, the QR-code check-in feature of an employee transportation app used by thousands of warehouse staff (including torch support for scanning in the dark).
- **Frontend architecture:** contributing to the move to Next.js (App Router) and TypeScript, reusable components in a monorepo with design-system packages and Storybook, and Persian/English with full RTL/LTR layouts.
- **Backend:** Fastify services with MongoDB, Redis and BullMQ: APIs, data models, scheduled jobs, and integrations with the HR/ERP system, Metabase and S3.
- **Quality:** Jest, Playwright and Cypress, plus JMeter for performance testing.
